PRIVACY

Last updated November 24, 2026

Capsule Vault is a free, unofficial fan project for tracking Dragon Ball S.H.Figuarts collections. This page explains in plain language what it stores, why, and how to get it deleted.

WITHOUT AN ACCOUNT

If you never sign in, everything you track stays in your own browser (local storage) on that device. Nothing about your collection is sent to us.

WHEN YOU SIGN IN

  • Your email address, used only to send you sign-in links. We don't send newsletters or marketing.
  • Your collection: the figures you mark, prices you log, notes, wishlist, badges and settings. This is private: database rules allow only your account to read or change it.
  • Photos you upload. These are stored in a public folder so showcase pages can display them. Anyone who has the exact address of a photo can view it, so don't upload anything personal.

YOUR PUBLIC SHOWCASE

A showcase only exists if you press PUBLISH. It shows your display name, the figures you own and their photos, plus values only if you choose. It never includes what you paid, your notes or your wishlist. Press TAKE DOWN to remove it at any time.

THE AI LAB

When you use Snap to ID, Bootleg Check or Ask Your Vault, the photo, listing text or question you provide, plus the collection details needed to answer, is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate the answer. It goes through our server, which records which account made each request and how much it used, so we can keep costs under control. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, API data isn't used to train their models.

SERVICES WE USE

  • Supabase: sign-in, database and photo storage
  • Netlify: hosts the website (standard server logs such as IP addresses)
  • Anthropic: the AI features, only when you use them

We don't sell your data, show ads or use tracking cookies.

DELETING YOUR DATA

You can remove figures, prices and photos inside the app, and take down your showcase anytime. To delete your whole account and everything stored with it, open a request on the project's GitHub issues page and we'll take care of it. Don't post your email address there; we'll reply on the issue to confirm.

KIDS

Capsule Vault isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too. Big changes will be noted on the main page.
